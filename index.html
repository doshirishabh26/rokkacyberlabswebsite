Tour Payment System
✦ New System

How Tour Billing Works — Rolling Weekly ACH Instalments

Unlike a single-event charge, tour billing creates one weekly commitment per crew member per work week — each anchored to that person's individual start date.

1
Offer Accepted → Anchor Set & Week 1 Committed

The moment crew accepts, the system locks in their anchor day = max(tour start, position start, hire date) and creates a Week 1 commitment. If the hire is inside the 7-day window, the card is charged immediately. Otherwise it queues for the next ACH batch.

⚠ No card on file at hire inside T-7? The hire is automatically rolled back and the company is notified.
2
Tour Starts → Week 2 Auto-Created

On the calendar day the tour begins, the platform automatically creates a Week 2 commitment for every hired crew member. Same ACH / card-escalation logic applies — no PM action required.

3
Every 7 Days from Anchor → Next Week Created by Cron

Weeks 3, 4, 5… are created by a daily cron only on each crew member's anchor-day anniversary — not all at once upfront. This prevents premature commitment creation for long tours. Mid-tour hires start at their own Week 1, not the tour's current week number.

4
Funding Due Date → ACH Batch Fires

Funding due = anchor + (week − 1) × 7 days — the start of that work week. When this date is within 7 days, the ACH batch fires. ACH typically settles in 2–4 business days.

5
Work Week Ends → 7-Day Hold

Once the work week completes, a 7-day hold begins before the payout transfer fires. This window allows for any disputes or corrections before money leaves Stripe.

✓
Crew Paid — No Wait for Job Completion

Payout eligible = anchor + week × 7 + 7 days. Each week pays out independently — crew don't wait until the tour wraps to see money.

✦ T&A has no effect on tour commitment amounts. Weekly pay is a flat contract — OT overruns and attendance discrepancies do not change the charge or payout for that week.

Per-Skill Weekly Tour Rate — Separate from Event Rate

Crew members set two independent rates per skill: an hourly/daily event rate (H/D) and a weekly tour rate ($/wk). These are completely separate fields — setting a tour rate does not affect the event rate and vice versa.

Offer Flow

Weekly-Only Pay Type for Tour Offers

The Send Offer dialog locks to Weekly when opening on a tour position — Hourly and Daily options are hidden. The rate pre-fills from the position's budget or the crew member's $/wk rate.

Once accepted, the offer immediately creates a Week 1 commitment and queues it for the next ACH batch — no further action needed from the company.

Batch Timing

When Does the Money Actually Move?

  • ACH pull — fires when a commitment's funding due date is within 7 days
  • Card charge — fires immediately if the hire happens inside the 7-day window
  • Settlement — ACH typically settles in 2–4 business days; funds move to Stripe balance
  • Crew payout — 7 days after the work week ends, Stripe Connect transfer fires to crew's bank account automatically
  • All of this is fully automated — companies set it up once and the platform handles every week

Scan-Based Daily Attendance (v2) — Replaces Legacy Timesheet

A new QR-scan-first attendance system built for the realities of live production:

  • Crew scan in/out via QR code on site — works cross-browser including Safari
  • PM can manually add or remove scans with a reason; full edit history is logged
  • Per-day KPI summary: on-time, late, no-show, overtime flags at a glance
  • Approaching OT banner with inline chips when crew are nearing the 10-hour mark mid-shift
  • Per-crew drawer shows full scan timeline, OT tier breakdown (regular → 1.5× → 2×), and meal/rest-break violations

✦ New

Hotel Provided for Crew — Set at Job Creation, Affects OT Calculations

A new "Hotel provided for crew" checkbox has been added to the Additional Details section of the job creation wizard (alongside Require US Passport, Dress Code, and Tags).

  • Signals to crew that accommodation is covered for out-of-town talent — shown prominently on the job listing to attract a wider radius of applicants
  • When checked, the short-turnaround rest threshold drops from 8 hours to 6 hours — crew earn overtime sooner on back-to-back show days because hotel guests are expected to commute faster
  • The setting flows through to the Time & Attendance tab automatically — no separate toggle needed on each attendance day
  • PMs can still override the hotel flag per individual crew member on the attendance tab if a specific person is commuting locally

Example: Jenny works Mon night until 2 AM, then is called back at 8 AM Tuesday. Without hotel: 6-hour gap > 8h threshold → no OT carryover. With hotel checked: 6-hour gap = exactly 6h threshold → short-turnaround kicks in → Tuesday OT clock starts earlier.

✦ New

Meal Break & Rest Break Violations

Automated violation detection built directly into pay calculation:

  • First and second meal break windows tracked per work block
  • 15-minute rest break required between hours 2–3 of each block
  • Violations shown inline on the PM's view; each violation adds 1.5× hourly to crew gross pay

Scan States & Valid Transitions

Every QR scan advances a state machine. The system enforces which scan type can legally follow the previous one — invalid transitions are rejected server-side. Auto-detect mode (the default) reads the crew member's last recorded state and pre-selects the only logical next step.

→ Site In Clock starts

Crew member arrives on site. Billable time begins from this timestamp.

From: none (first scan)  ·  site_out (re-entry)
Next: Break out  ·  Provided meal  ·  Site out
⏸ Break Out Clock pauses

Crew leaves for an unpaid break. Billable hours stop accumulating. Meal-break window opens.

From: site_in  ·  break_in  ·  provided_meal
Next: Break in  ·  Site out
▶ Break In Clock resumes

Crew returns from break. Billable hours resume. The break duration is excluded from the day total.

From: break_out
Next: Break out  ·  Provided meal  ·  Site out
☕ Provided Meal Clock running

Company provided an on-site meal. Crew stays on the clock for 30 min — satisfies the meal-break obligation without pausing pay.

From: site_in  ·  break_in  ·  provided_meal
Next: Break out  ·  Provided meal  ·  Site out
← Site Out Clock stops

Crew leaves for the day. Final billable total is locked. A second Site In is allowed if crew returns later.

From: site_in  ·  break_out  ·  break_in  ·  provided_meal
Next: Site in (re-entry only)

Manual Correction — PM adds or fixes a scan after the fact
When to use: crew forgot to scan in/out, or a scan was recorded at the wrong time. The PM selects a scan type, sets the corrected date & time, and optionally adds a reason for the audit trail.

Every manual correction is logged in editHistory[] on the time entry — field, old value, new value, who corrected it, and when.
Confirmed entries (status: confirmed) are locked and cannot be overwritten by a QR scan — manual correction is the only path for already-confirmed rows. This prevents accidental clock-out re-opens after a PM has signed off.
Scan Type Picker — Manual correction dropdown
The same 5 scan types used by QR scanning are available here for manual entry. The state-machine rules still apply server-side even for manual corrections — you can't add a Break in if the last scan isn't a Break out.
